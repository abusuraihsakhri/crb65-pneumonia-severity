CRB-65 Pneumonia Severity Calculator

For adults with clinically diagnosed community-acquired pneumonia in primary care.

Clinical scope: CRB-65 supports clinical judgement; it does not replace it. Refer to hospital regardless of score when there are signs of a more serious illness such as cardiorespiratory failure or sepsis. This tool does not prescribe antibiotics.

Enter all measured values, then calculate the score.

Privacy: calculations run entirely in your browser. This page does not transmit or store entered values.

Guidance basis: NICE NG250: Pneumonia — diagnosis and management. The score uses 1 point each for confusion, respiratory rate ≥30/min, systolic BP <90 mmHg or diastolic BP ≤60 mmHg, and age ≥65 years.
